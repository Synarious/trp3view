Import Profile

How to export your TRP3 Profile:

  1. Open Total RP 3 in-game with "/trp3 open" without a target.
  2. Click "Profiles" on the left sidebar.
  3. Find your profile and click the More actions button (the gear/menu icon).
  4. Select Export profile (Warning: Do not click delete!).
  5. Copy the entire string provided and paste it into the box below.
